Privacy Policy

PDF & Image Toolkit (com.pdfimagetoolkit.app)
Last updated: September 29, 2026

This policy explains what information PDF & Image Toolkit ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Your files stay on your device

All PDF and image work, including creating, merging, splitting, compressing, editing, resizing and cropping, happens entirely on your phone. The files you open and create are never uploaded to us or to any server. We cannot see, read or store your documents or photos.

The app only opens files you choose with the Android file picker. Files you save go to the Download/PDF Image Toolkit folder on your device, or to a location you pick. On Android 9 and older, the app asks for storage permission only to save files to that folder.

No account, no personal details

The app has no sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or precise location.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. Ads are only requested when your device is online.

We do not collect, store or share any information ourselves. Any information used to show ads is collected and processed by Google, not by this app, under the Google Privacy Policy. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from the app's settings.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads on some phones).

Turning off mobile data and Wi-Fi stops all ads. The app's tools keep working offline.

App updates

The app uses Google Play's in-app update service to tell you when a new version is available. This check is handled by Google Play and does not share your files.

Data retention and deletion

We do not run servers and do not keep any of your data. Files you create stay on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes its temporary files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Because your files never leave your device, they are protected by your phone's own security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us here.
